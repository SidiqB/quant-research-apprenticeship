## Evidence and roadmap decisions

1. Python and statistical work recur throughout the sample. Build a clean Python package, provenance and
chronological evaluation before complex models. This sequencing is a research judgement, not an employer prescription.
2. AQR's data scientist posting names data validation, Git, PyTest and CI/CD; Jane Street's research engineer role
emphasises reproducible code and production research infrastructure. Shared infrastructure is therefore a substantive milestone.
3. Point72 microstructure research, DV order-book research and AQR execution analytics connect market structure,
tick data and execution costs. Reserve a dedicated simulator phase; use accounting identities before profitability studies.
4. Options trading and risk postings support Greeks, volatility and scenario analysis. Pricing benchmarks and parity
checks precede hedging studies. Senior risk work cannot be replicated by merely computing a risk metric.
5. C++ and low-latency engineering appear in technology roles, but Python also spans research and risk.
For production acceleration, introduce C++ only after profiling demonstrates a bottleneck and a Python
reference is validated. Sidiq's October 4 curriculum makes C++ the default for new educational projects without a speedup
requirement. C001/C002 are complete; C003a parsing comes next, slowly, with Python verification.
This user preference does not change the dated posting counts or imply current vacancy status.

## Personal fit and gaps

The supplied mathematics background and Python experience are relevant foundations, not verified evidence that
all employer requirements have been met. Research logs, independent reproduction and explainable failure cases
should establish the next layer of evidence. Communication is practised through learning notes and weekly synthesis.
Student graduation windows, commercial experience and sponsorship restrictions are material filters. Keep
experienced roles as skill benchmarks; do not represent this programme as a substitute for professional tenure.
Citi explicitly requests independent pricing benchmarks, numerical accuracy checks and model-risk reports. This supports the risk phase, but one bank role cannot represent the whole model-validation labour market.
