# Role research methodology

Access date: 21 September 2026. Sample: 49 distinct public employer postings across eight firms.
Sources: 48 employer-hosted Greenhouse listings and one directly opened Citi career posting. Each row links its individual source.
Job descriptions were retrieved in full; requirements and eligibility sections were inspected, with keyword coding of the full text. Only concise factual coding is redistributed.
SHA-256 hashes identify the normalised source text used; these are fingerprints, not proof of future availability.

## Sampling and interpretation

Purposive sampling covers research, trading, systematic investing, development, technology, execution,
risk and investment data science, with graduate and experienced roles. London roles were included where available.
Identical company/title families across locations were excluded; materially different teams, instruments or seniority
were retained. The selected 49 have unique company/title pairs and unique posting URLs.
This is a representative functional sample, not a random census of hiring or a claim of personal eligibility.
US roles and eight firms dominate the available feeds; do not infer UK-wide demand or hiring probabilities.
A live board listing on the access date is the definition of current; it does not guarantee active interviewing.
No job applications were submitted.

## Field semantics

The requested required_mathematics, required_statistics and required_programming_languages column names
are retained for compatibility. Their values are explicit topic/language mentions in the job description;
mentions can be mandatory, preferred, examples, degree subjects or duties. They are NOT a count of mandatory
requirements. Always consult the linked role for modality. Not explicitly found means no matching term was
captured, not that the employer does not value the skill. Finance terms likewise can describe duties.
Experience, degree and eligibility fields are concise paraphrases of the descriptions.
Recurring-keyword counts are descriptive text frequencies, not rankings of importance or hiring success.

Skills already demonstrated are based solely on the supplied background; no independent portfolio audit was
performed. Repository development skills are editorial inferences, separated from employer evidence.
No citizenship, visa status or GPA conversion is inferred. Several student roles require future graduation;
already having graduated may preclude eligibility. Confirm directly before any future application.
One Citi credit-derivatives validation role adds model-risk coverage; this small subgroup is not representative of all bank validation work. Broaden that subgroup at the next refresh.

## Refresh protocol

Revisit links monthly, record closed/replaced postings, preserve original access dates and do not silently overwrite
historical evidence. Add a separately dated cohort if the sample changes. Review explicit required versus preferred
wording before using counts for stronger conclusions. Keep job text out of Git unless redistribution is licensed.
