# Research charter

Build three connected research laboratories through 31 December 2026: systematic equity signals,
market microstructure and execution, and derivatives/volatility/risk. Shared validation comes first.
The programme is educational research, with no live trading or investment recommendation.

## Research principles

State questions and hypotheses before results. Preserve negative findings and all attempted configurations.
Separate engineering validation, synthetic demonstrations and empirical claims. A profitable backtest is not proof of alpha.
Track information availability, label intervals, membership history, corporate actions and revisions explicitly.
Use chronological holdouts, simple baselines, uncertainty estimates and multiple-testing controls.
Model turnover, spreads, commissions, impact and execution delay before discussing investability.
Explain assumptions and failure cases alongside metrics. Add complexity only against a tested baseline.
Use Python first; profile before introducing C++. Reusable logic belongs in the package.
Never invent qualifications, review status, data, research activity or performance.

## Review

Daily notes record Sidiq review status, initially Not yet reviewed. Only Sidiq can confirm a transition to
Reviewed conceptually, Reproduced independently or Extended independently. Progress does not wait for review.
Descriptions concern repository outputs, not unverified claims about personal expertise.
