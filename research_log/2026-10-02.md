## Research question and hypothesis

exit? The hypothesis is that stable security identities and explicit entry/exit boundaries preserve

## Beginner explanation and definitions

Imagine reconstructing a school's class list for January. Using only the students still enrolled in
June would erase those who left. It might also add students who had not yet joined. Research universes
have the same timing problem. Keep dated membership records instead of applying today's roster to
every historical observation.

**Universe:** the set of eligible security identities under a declared rule. **Stable security ID:**
a key intended to follow one security, independent of its display ticker. A company and a security
are different levels of identity; one company can have multiple securities. **Effective date:** when
a membership change applies under the source convention. **Entry:** first included date. **Exit:**
first excluded date. **Re-entry:** a new membership interval after an earlier interval ends.

**Coverage:** the date range for which the caller asserts a complete history. **Survivor filter:**
restricting a past population to identities present at a later date. **As-known-at history:** records
limited to information available by a decision time. Effective dates alone do not establish this.

## Data, metrics and evaluation protocol

All four identities and five intervals are synthetic. Coverage is January 1 through January 8, 2026;
January 9 is the exclusive end. These are calendar-date labels, including non-session days, chosen
to expose boundaries. They are not market observations or an exchange-session sample.

The evaluation uses eight explicit hand membership sets, an intentionally wrong final-date filter,
invalid-input controls, re-entry and adjacency cases, an independent event ledger and deterministic
subprocess replay. Membership comparisons are exact set/tuple comparisons with no numeric tolerance.
Full project checks and a verified PDF are required. No empirical performance or alpha is estimated.

## Mathematics and boundary convention

For security i with intervals indexed by j, define M_i(d)=1 if any interval satisfies
entry_ij <= d < exit_ij, and M_i(d)=0 otherwise. With no finite exit, membership continues only within
declared coverage. The universe is U(d)={i : M_i(d)=1}, evaluated only when start <= d < end.
Outside that range the answer is unknown and the API raises an error.

The half-open convention includes the entry date and excludes the exit date. It avoids counting
two adjacent intervals twice: [Jan 1, Jan 3) followed by [Jan 3, Jan 5) includes Jan 3 exactly once.
Same-ID overlaps are rejected rather than silently merged. A gap means nonmembership under the
completeness assertion. Different security IDs may be eligible at the same time.

## Deliberately wrong survivor control

At d=Jan 2, U(d)={OLD, RETURN, STAY}. At T=Jan 8, U(T)={NEW, RETURN, STAY}.
The wrong filter U(d) intersect U(T) returns {RETURN, STAY}: OLD is wrongly removed. One of three
historical members is lost. Copying U(T) directly would also include NEW before its entry.

No returns are attached to these identities. We therefore demonstrate the selection error without
claiming a positive or negative performance bias. An exit can reflect many events; it is not itself
a measured loss, liquidation instruction or zero recovery. A position ledger must retain holdings
until supported accounting events resolve them.

## Implementation and reproduction

src/quant_research/data/membership.py adds immutable MembershipInterval and MembershipHistory records.
The history canonicalizes a tuple of records and members_on returns sorted immutable identity tuples.
IDs reject blank values, whitespace and control characters. Entry, exit, coverage and query dates
reject datetimes and strings; a datetime is a date subclass, so an exact date-type check is deliberate.
No time or zone is discarded silently. Exit is required; None explicitly asserts continued membership.

Intervals must have positive duration and intersect coverage. They may extend outside its boundaries.
Duplicate or overlapping intervals for one ID fail in either input order; adjacency and re-entry pass.
An empty tuple explicitly declares an empty universe within coverage. The model cannot authenticate
that declaration. Construction sorts in O(n log n); a query scans intervals and sorts selected IDs.

scripts/membership_experiment.py records the interval inputs, eight date sets and the wrong filter
in experiments/membership/results.json. The experiment README documents the complete contract and
limitations. Run make membership-experiment, make check and make note NOTE_DATE=2026-10-02.

## Limitations and interview explanation

This is a retrospective effective-date model. It lacks announcement times, revisions, processing
latency and source vintages. A later correction to an earlier interval can still change past results.
An interval history removes a current-roster error but does not prove point-in-time information safety.
Q004a's licensed evidence audit and Q005b's provenance integration remain prerequisites for empirical use.

Coverage and stable identity are caller assertions. The API cannot detect a missing historical member,
an incorrect ticker mapping or an unjustified open-ended interval. Missing exit evidence must not be
converted to None unless continuation through coverage is established. Missing records are not proof
of nonmembership. Completeness must come from audited upstream evidence.

Dates have no timezone or execution timestamp. Map exchange-local decision dates and source-specific
before-open/after-close conventions upstream; this API does not validate sessions, tradeability,
liquidity, price availability or fills. No market data, dependency, portfolio ledger or multi-universe
framework was added. No performance or speedup claim is supported by this fixture.

For an interview, first distinguish effective membership, knowledge at the decision and actual holdings.
Then state the interval boundaries, demonstrate OLD's exclusion by the wrong filter and explain unknown
coverage. Finish by identifying what remains unproved: the history's completeness, publication vintage
and terminal proceeds. Repository completion is separate from personal mastery or independent reproduction.
