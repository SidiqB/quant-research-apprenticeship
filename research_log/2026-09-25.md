## Research question and hypothesis

Can a data source support historical stock selection, return accounting and information timing under our
actual access permissions? The hypothesis is that advertised inactive-stock coverage is insufficient:
unknown access, missing terminal-event evidence or unavailable historical vintages must block admission.

## Beginner explanation and terms

Imagine studying a school using only the pupils still enrolled at graduation. Those who left are missing
from the earlier classes too. A current stock list can make the same mistake: companies that disappeared
are excluded from the history even though they were available to buy at the time.

**Universe:** the securities eligible for consideration at a decision time. **Survivorship bias:** selection
based on later survival. **Delisting:** removal from a trading venue, not necessarily economic worthlessness.
**Permanent identifier:** a security identity that survives ticker changes. **Vintage:** the version of data
available at a particular time. **Entitlement:** the actual permission to access a product for a stated use.

Historical coverage and historical availability are separate. A corrected 2012 price retrieved today is
an observation about 2012, but that corrected value may not have been available during 2012. An accurate
checksum of today's file cannot prove an old trader knew its contents.

## Provisional universe and mathematics

The first price-based study targets USD-quoted common shares of operating/holding companies on NYSE,
Nasdaq and NYSE American, including REIT common shares. Qualifying share classes retain separate permanent
security IDs. ADRs, funds, preferreds, units, partnerships, warrants, blank-check companies and OTC-only
listings are excluded. Historical classification is required; today's classification cannot be projected back.

Decision time is provisionally 09:00 America/New_York on a trading session. Use only available prior-session
inputs. The prior raw close must be at least USD 5. Mean close times share volume over the 20 prior sessions
must be at least USD 1 million per session. Missing inputs exclude a new entry with a recorded reason.
These are untuned teaching defaults, not a claim about executable liquidity or strategy profitability.

Let H_i(t) describe eligible historical listing/type and A_i(t) require information available by t.
Let L_i(t) = sum(P_i(s) * V_i(s) over 20 prior sessions s) / 20. Then eligibility is
E_i(t) = H_i(t) AND A_i(t) AND [P_i(d-1) >= 5] AND [L_i(t) >= 1000000].
P is unadjusted USD close and V is consolidated shares traded; their product is a turnover proxy.
All inputs must be known. Eligibility loss does not erase an existing holding or establish its exit price.

The complete rule is in data/universe-and-access.md. Q006 will implement the calendar and decision-time
contract; Q009 will implement membership. These components and executable portfolio rules are not built today.

## Worked example: three initial holdings

Each synthetic holding starts with 100 units. There are no intervening cash flows or costs. C's zero is an
explicitly confirmed zero recovery in the fixture, never a default for a missing real price.

| Holding | Initial value | Final value | Later status |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 100 | 120 | Survives |
| B | 100 | 90 | Survives |
| C | 100 | 0 | Disappears after confirmed loss |

The full population return is (120 + 90 + 0) / 300 - 1 = -0.30, or -30%.
A survivors-only history reports (120 + 90) / 200 - 1 = 0.05, or +5%.
The selection difference is 0.05 - (-0.30) = 0.35, or 35 percentage points.

This comparison changes the population and initial capital consistently. It demonstrates selection error,
not its empirical size. An acquired stock can leave with a gain, so the sign need not always be positive.

## Implementation and checks

The reusable src/quant_research/data/access.py checker requires eight gates: licence entitlement, local
access, historical identity, inactive coverage, corporate actions, terminal events, availability vintages
and permitted outputs. Each has a status and a nonblank evidence reference. Unknown fields, omissions,
malformed records and invalid statuses fail validation. Blocker order is deterministic.

Only verified passes. Documented is a vendor claim; unknown is unresolved; failed records contradictory
evidence for this contract. These three statuses all block. The two dated real-candidate records contain
no verified gates. The checklist checks declarations; it does not authenticate references or permissions,
fetch data, enforce ingestion or validate security membership. A dishonest declaration can pass it.

Run make access-experiment to reproduce the dated candidate assessment, positive control and all negative
controls. Run make check for the full suite. Run make note NOTE_DATE=2026-09-25 to rebuild this PDF.
Tests compare the arithmetic to literal expected values and compare the result file byte-for-byte.

## Limitations and interview explanation

No source is empirically admitted, no market coverage is measured, and no live investment recommendation
is made. A hash proves neither licensing nor historical completeness. Today's checker reports human evidence
assertions; it is not a permission service or an ingestion barrier. Future code must enforce its own contracts.
Strict historical receipt/vintage requirements may require a different source or a documented narrower claim.

In an interview, explain the changing population before describing returns. Show the three-holding arithmetic,
then distinguish a missing price from a confirmed loss. Explain why inactive coverage, historical membership,
revision timing and permission are separate requirements. Finish with what the implementation actually checks.

## Suggested answers

1. It conditions the past sample on later survival, excluding securities that were then eligible. Our toy
sample changes from -30% to +5%, but real selection effects need not have that magnitude or sign.

2. No. A company may migrate venue or be acquired. A terminal return requires proceeds and payment evidence;
missing proceeds are unresolved, whereas the fixture explicitly specifies zero recovery.

3. A vendor statement establishes neither actual permission nor successful sample verification in this
project. Every requirement needs project evidence; a nonblank reference alone does not establish truth.

4. No. Delaying a revised value does not recover the missing original vintage. The claim must remain blocked
or be explicitly narrowed before research using the revised history.
